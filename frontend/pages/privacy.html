Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 July 2026

This policy explains how KenadHR handles personal data in the HR workspace. It is written for employees, applicants, and the organisations that use KenadHR.

1. Who controls your information

Your employer or organisation generally decides why and how employee data is used in KenadHR and is the data controller. KenadHR provides the software and processes that data on the organisation’s documented instructions. For KenadHR account, billing, support, and security information, KenadHR may act as a controller.

2. Information processed

Depending on the features your organisation enables, we process account and profile details, employment and payroll information, attendance records, approved leave, uploaded documents, messages, notifications, device/browser information, and precise location only when a staff member clocks in or out. We do not collect payment-card details; Paystack processes payments for subscriptions.

3. Why we use it

We use data to provide and secure the service, manage employee records and access, process attendance, leave and payroll workflows, send requested notifications, provide support, meet legal obligations, and improve reliability. Location data is used only for the attendance action that requested it and is visible to authorised HR users.

4. Sharing and international processing

We share information with your organisation’s authorised users, service providers needed to operate the platform (including hosting, communications, and payment providers), and authorities where legally required. We do not sell personal data. If data is processed outside Ghana, we will use appropriate safeguards required by applicable law.

5. Retention and security

We retain data for the period required to provide the service, follow the customer’s retention instructions, resolve disputes, and meet legal or accounting obligations. We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, including authenticated access and role-based permissions. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Your choices and rights

You may ask your organisation to access, correct, update, or delete your personal data, or to object to particular processing where applicable. If you cannot resolve a concern with your organisation, you may contact the relevant data-protection authority. Ghana’s Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) provides rights including access and objection.

7. Children and changes

KenadHR is intended for workplace use and not for children. We may update this policy as the service or legal requirements change; the latest version will be published here with its updated date.

8. Contact

For questions about employee data, contact your employer’s HR team first. For KenadHR platform privacy questions, contact Adusei Media at hello@aduseimedia.codes or 0554266661.
